# Cluster Analysis

## 🎯 Grouping Similar Observations

Find natural groups in data based on similarity.

---

## 📊 Goal

Group observations so that:
- **Within-cluster similarity** is HIGH
- **Between-cluster similarity** is LOW

---

## 🔄 Cluster vs. Factor Analysis

| Aspect | Cluster | Factor |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Groups** | Observations (rows) | Variables (columns) |
| **Output** | Group membership | Underlying dimensions |
| **Example** | Customer segments | Survey dimensions |

---

## 🎨 Methods

### Hierarchical Clustering
- Builds tree (dendrogram)
- Agglomerative (bottom-up)

### K-Means Clustering
- Specify k clusters
- Iteratively assigns to nearest centroid

---

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **Cluster**: Group similar observations
2. **Factor**: Group similar variables
3. **Hierarchical**: Tree structure
4. **K-means**: Pre-specify k
5. **Goal**: Maximize within-cluster similarity
