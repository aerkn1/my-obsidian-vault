# Standard Error and Confidence Intervals

## 🎯 From Sample to Population

How confident are we in our estimates?

---

## 📊 Standard Error

### Definition
**Standard deviation of the sampling distribution**

### Formula
**SE** = σ / √n

Where:
- σ = population SD
- n = sample size

### Interpretation
"Typical distance between sample mean and population mean"

### Key Insight
**Larger n → Smaller SE → More precise estimate**

**To halve SE, quadruple n!**

---

## 📐 Confidence Intervals

### Definition
**Range likely to contain population parameter**

### Formula
**95% CI** = x̄ ± 1.96 × SE

### Interpretation
"We are 95% confident the true population mean is in this range"

### Example
- Sample mean = 100
- SE = 5
- 95% CI = 100 ± 1.96(5) = [90.2, 109.8]

---

## 🎚️ Confidence Levels

| Confidence Level | Critical Value |
|------------------|----------------|
| 90% | ±1.645 |
| 95% | ±1.96 |
| 99% | ±2.576 |

**Higher confidence → Wider interval**

---

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **SE** = σ/√n (measures precision)
2. **SE decreases** as n increases
3. **95% CI** = x̄ ± 1.96(SE)
4. **Higher confidence** = wider interval
5. **Larger sample** = narrower interval
