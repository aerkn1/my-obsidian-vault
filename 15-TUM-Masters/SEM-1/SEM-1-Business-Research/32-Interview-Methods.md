# Interview Methods and Techniques

## 🎯 The Art of Asking

Interviews provide rich, detailed data about experiences and perspectives.

---

## 🎨 Types

### Structured
- Fixed questions
- Fixed order
- Like oral survey

### Semi-Structured
- Interview guide
- Flexibility to probe
- Most common

### Unstructured
- Open conversation
- General topic
- Maximum flexibility

---

## ✅ Advantages

✅ Depth and nuance
✅ Understand rationales
✅ Flexibility to probe
✅ Good for sensitive topics

---

## ❌ Disadvantages

❌ Subjective responses
❌ Social desirability bias
❌ Time-consuming
❌ Hard to analyze
❌ Not generalizable

---

## 🎯 Best Practices

### Before
- Prepare guide
- Research participants
- Test recording equipment

### During
- Build rapport
- Open-ended questions
- Active listening
- Avoid leading questions
- Probe for details

### After
- Transcribe promptly
- Member checking
- Begin analysis

---

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **Interviews**: Rich qualitative data
2. **Semi-structured**: Most flexible yet systematic
3. **Subjective**: Watch for biases
4. **Time-intensive**: Plan accordingly
5. **Transcribe**: Essential for analysis
